## MIT Sloan COO Program

Completed the Chief Operating Officer (COO) Program at MIT, focusing on operating model design, transformation leadership, and strategic decision-making at global scale.

### Focus Areas
- **System Dynamics**: Modeling complex organization dynamics.
- **Operational Excellence**: Applying disciplined operating rhythms, feedback loops, and execution governance.
- **Enterprise Transformation**: Connecting operating strategy with technology-enabled change.
