# Interactive To-Do List

Boost your daily productivity with our interactive, high-fidelity To-Do List application. Formulate clear goals, track subtask progress, set priorities, and stay focused with integrated Pomodoro timers.

## Core Features

- **Categorized Tasks**: Organize your workload into distinct projects or personal spaces.
- **Priority Management**: Color-coded badges for Critical, High, Medium, and Low priorities.
- **Progress Tracking**: Real-time progress bars linked directly to subtask checklists.
- **Focus Timer**: Work in focused intervals using the built-in customizable Pomodoro timer.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamless translation between English and Japanese locales.
- **Local Persistence**: State automatically saved to localStorage for seamless persistence.
